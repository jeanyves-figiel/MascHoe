## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Database backups with point-in-time restore** (infra, SR-01 #141): the Postgres image now runs pgBackRest. Every change is copied off the DB machine within 5 min (WAL archive) and a backup is taken daily (full weekly, differential otherwise), encrypted before upload to a private Tigris bucket per environment (`thirstr-backups-<env>`), kept ~5 weeks. Restore to any second in that window: runbook `docs/runbooks/db-backups.md` (staging drill passed 2026-11-21). DB machines stop cleanly (SIGINT, 30 s). Infra images workflow can target one environment and tags images by commit.
- **Enterprise readiness plan** (docs): `docs/spec/enterprise-readiness.md`: targets (availability, latency, data loss, battery and data use), workstreams (HA database, regions, SLOs/on-call, incidents, security, compliance incl. DSA, canary releases, feature flags, chaos tests, DR drills, performance/battery/data budgets, support, moderation, costs), phased roadmap and monthly cost at 10k / 100k / 1M users. Issues EN-01 … EN-22 (#160–#181).
- **Scalability & reliability review** (docs, 2026-11-21): `docs/spec/scalability-and-reliability.md` covers growth limits (DB, Explore queries, photos, machines, Oban, regions), whether messages can be lost (in transit: no; waiting on the server or kept over time: gaps) and what the phone caches and encrypts. 16 prioritized follow-ups SR-01 … SR-16 (#141–#156); top: Postgres backups + PITR, metrics/alerts, push retries, graceful deploys, keep queued messages on device revocation, keep chat photos after 30 days.
- **Sending photos and albums in chat, redone** (#135, F-08 R-08.11–R-08.16, [mockup option A](https://claude.ai/artifact/XRKiLgLwVEyKAgdnYypj5X)): the chat **+** now opens a media tray where the keyboard was, so the chat stays readable (the old "Send a photo" sheet dimmed it to blank). **Photos**: a "Sent recently" row of your last 30 sent photos (one tap to send again; "Sent here" marks what this chat already got), your camera roll (iOS "selected photos" access supported, Manage access) and the camera; pick up to 10, set ⏱ expiry / 👁 once / ⤓ save in the bar, **Send N**. Options are remembered; Safe Mode and Auto-delete keep 👁 Once on. **Albums**: your private albums with a SHARED timer when shared in this chat, share sheet with 1 h / 24 h / 7 d / Until I stop, Change time and Stop sharing, and New private album right from the chat. Shared albums show as a card in the chat (preview, photo count, time left; Open album for him, Stop sharing for you) instead of a text line. Privacy: Sent recently photos stay only on the phone, encrypted with their own keys; sending one again uploads a new encrypted copy; clear them in Settings › Chats & media; sign-out and wipes delete them. Camera roll photos are re-encoded on the phone (no EXIF/GPS) before sending. Videos follow in #138.
- **Places import from GitHub Actions** (#113, F-12 R-12.9): new manual workflow "Places import" (staging or production) queues the OpenStreetMap venue import on the server (same job as admin › Places › Import now), waits for it and shows added / updated / removed and the venue count in the run summary. Fixed: the first version never queued the job (the start flag didn't reach the server) and waited until cancelled. A deploy during an import no longer leaves it stuck: interrupted import jobs are rescued after 45 min (Oban Lifeline), and the workflow cancels jobs killed by a restart before queuing a new one.

### Fixed
- **Map: one tap opens a guy's profile; zooming in no longer empties the map** (#113, F-04 R-04.2, R-04.14): tapping a ringed pin opens the profile right away instead of first showing a peek card. Hold a pin (right-click on web) for Hide · Block · Report. When zoomed in closer than one area cell, the people and heat in that cell disappeared because the server only counted cells whose centre was on screen; it now includes every cell that touches the screen.
- **Nav bar on every screen** (app, #137): the bottom nav bar (Explore · Tonight · Chats · Me) now stays visible on all screens, also profiles, chats, settings and forms, not only on the four main tabs. The tab you came from stays lit; tapping any tab closes the open screens and goes there. Chat's message box, the profile buttons and Save / Send buttons sit just above it. It hides while the keyboard is up (and on the selfie camera).
- **Keyboard: no gap above it, swipe down closes it** (iPhone, #134): in a chat the message box sat ~40 pt above the keyboard (the home-indicator space was kept while the keyboard covered it); same in chat search and onboarding buttons. Swiping the messages (and any form or sheet) now closes the keyboard. Fields in bottom sheets and the sign-up email field move up with the keyboard instead of hiding behind it; long forms (Edit profile, Report, party / activity / club, settings) scroll so the field you type in stays visible.
- **Selfie verification always failed** (iPhone, #131, F-03 R-03.1): Look up could never pass and head turns rarely did, because Apple Vision's landmark check alone uses an older face detector (no head tilt, head turn in 45° steps); blinks were checked on 3 still photos and almost never caught. The check now uses Vision's newer face detector (smooth turn and tilt) with a backup from the nose position, keeps taking photos for up to 6 s per step until the move is seen, shows a ✓ after each step, and no longer asks to blink. If the phone can't see your face at the start, it says so and the try doesn't count. Photos are still checked on the phone and deleted at once.
- **Text no longer overlaps or hides behind other elements** (app, #124): on a profile, "Verify to send the first message" was a see-through banner floating over the Private note; it is now a solid toast above the action bar with a **Verify now** button (also from Explore's quick Message). Profile content fades out under the action bar and always scrolls clear of it on every phone. Tab-style switches (Chats All / Unread / Favorites / Friends and others) size each tab to its label so long French and German words fit on small phones. Screens checked with an automated overlap scan on 320, 375 and 393 pt wide phones in en / fr / de.
- **Screen froze after cancelling Share** (iPhone, #124): Share profile (profile ⋯), Send link (Me › QR) and inviting a trusted contact opened the system share sheet on top of a closing menu; after Cancel the invisible menu kept every touch. The menu now closes first and the share sheet opens once it is gone.
- **App kept signing you out / chat always "Waiting for network"** (#116, R-01.5): after 15 min idle the access token expires and several requests refreshed it at the same time with the same refresh token. The server saw the second use as a stolen token and deleted the device (sign-in, chat keys and pending messages gone), so the app signed out and realtime was refused. The app now refreshes once and shares the result, and signs out only on a definitive 401/403 (not offline, 409, 5xx). Server: re-use of a refresh token spent less than 60 s ago returns 409 `refresh_race` without revoking; older re-use still revokes the device. Concurrent refresh no longer crashes with a 500.
- **Map now opens on your position** (#95, R-04.13): the Explore map stayed zoomed out on the world because it only read the position when it first appeared, before the phone had a fix. It now centres on your own position as soon as it is known (whatever your location level: Hidden, City, Area or Exact), asks for location access once if never asked, and keeps the map where you put it if you already moved it. New "Locate me" button recentres. Your exact position never leaves the phone. `GET /me` returns your own coarse cell (`location.cell_r5`) so City users without location access start on their city.
- **iPhone app stuck on launch** (#90): startup waited forever when reading the saved sign-in from the keychain or reaching the server failed or hung. Each startup step now has a time limit and falls through to the Welcome screen. A failed check no longer signs you out unless the server rejects the session.
- **iPhone app still opened to an empty screen** (#88): the screen frame waited for the phone to report its safe-area insets before drawing anything. It now starts from known measurements. Errors during startup are shown on screen instead of nothing. `expo-asset` (needed by fonts) is now a direct dependency.
- **Chat works on bad networks** (app, #76, #79): every outgoing message, reaction and receipt goes to an encrypted on-device outbox before sending and is retried automatically (1 s → 60 s backoff), also after the app is killed. Reconnect, launch and foreground send at once. Gives up after 24 h with "Not sent. Tap to retry." (same message, never a duplicate). Requests time out (15 s send, 10 s pull/ack) and the chat header shows "Waiting for network" when the connection is down.
- **No duplicates on retry** (app, #78): the message is encrypted once; retries resend the same ciphertext with `client_id`.
- **No silently lost messages** (app, #80): a message that can't be decrypted yet is kept up to 24 h and retried after each welcome/commit (e.g. it arrived before its chat setup); then a "couldn't be decrypted" notice is shown and the 1:1 chat re-syncs its keys. Messages expired on the server while away show a notice with the count. Pull acks only after everything is saved; re-deliveries after a kill aren't processed twice.
- **Chats keep all messages** (app, #81): one encrypted row per message instead of one blob capped at 500; writes to a chat are serialized so a send and a receive at the same time can't overwrite each other. Existing chats migrate on first open. Messages sort by sender time clamped to server time.
- **Chat send can't duplicate or half-deliver** (server, #78): `POST /mls/messages` takes `client_id`; a repeat from the same device within 7 d returns the first result and stores/pushes nothing. All envelopes of one call are stored in one transaction (all-or-nothing); `message.new` goes out after commit.
